What this page shows
One line is recorded here for every sign-in and sign-out, for every page a user opens, and for every record a user adds, saves, edits or deletes (who, when, which page, which record). Only you (Super Admin) can read it, and nobody can change or delete a line from the website. Lines older than 90 days are not shown.
Not recorded: wrong-password attempts, IP addresses, automatic sign-outs after inactivity, chat messages, and the contents of a record (only its name/number and the names of the fields changed). Several saves made within 3 seconds are shown as one line with a count. Page visits and data changes are recorded from the day this update went live.
